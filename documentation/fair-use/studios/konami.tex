
\subsection{Konami}

\subsubsection{Official Policy}

Konami's \textit{Suikoden I \& II Video Posting and
Copyrighted Work Using Guidelines}, revised June 29,
2026, explicitly permit gameplay videos, livestreams,
and certain original fan-created works.

The policy covers both the original PlayStation games
and their modern remasters, permitting edited gameplay
and live commentary under specified conditions.

Official policy:
\url{https://www.konami.com/games/suikoden/rem/1_2/us/en-us/ugc_guideline}

\subsubsection{Applicability to Silima}

We believe Silima is consistent with the broadcasting
activities recognized by Konami for the following reasons:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Broadcasting Platform:}
    Silima transmits gameplay to connected viewers,
    functioning similarly to established livestreaming
    platforms recognized by Konami.

    \item \textbf{Creative Contribution:}
    Collective voting and shared controller inputs
    produce gameplay shaped by audience decisions,
    creating an original interactive experience.

    \item \textbf{Interactive Commentary:}
    Participant reactions, emojis, and predefined
    messages provide an additional layer of social
    interaction and commentary alongside gameplay.

    \item \textbf{Noncommercial Use:}
    Silima does not charge participants or monetize
    gameplay broadcasts.
\end{itemize}

We believe these characteristics align with Konami's
recognition of interactive gameplay broadcasting and
original fan-created content.

However, Konami's guidelines are title-specific,
identify permitted streaming services, and expressly
exclude corporate and other group customers.
Consequently, they do not independently authorize
school-operated broadcasts through Silima.
