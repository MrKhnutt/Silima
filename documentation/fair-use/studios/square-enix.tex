
\subsection{Square Enix}

\subsubsection{Official Policy}

Square Enix publishes game-specific broadcasting
guidelines, including those for \textit{Chrono Trigger}
and the \textit{Final Fantasy Pixel Remaster} series.

These policies permit qualifying gameplay livestreams,
including commentary and other original contributions,
subject to restrictions on commercial use, attribution,
and distribution.

Relevant policies:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \href{
        https://www.square-enix-games.com/en_US/documents/chrono-trigger-guidelines-for-livestreaming-and-posting-videos-images
    }{Chrono Trigger Broadcasting Guidelines}

    \item \href{
        https://finalfantasypixelremaster.square-enix-games.com/en_US/streaming-guidelines
    }{Final Fantasy Pixel Remaster Guidelines}
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Applicability to Silima}

We believe Silima is consistent with the broadcasting
activities recognized by Square Enix for the following
reasons:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Broadcasting Platform:}
    Silima is designed to transmit gameplay to connected
    viewers, functioning similarly to conventional
    gameplay streaming services.

    \item \textbf{Creative Contribution:}
    Participants collectively determine gameplay through
    voting and shared inputs, creating an experience
    shaped by audience decisions.

    \item \textbf{Interactive Commentary:}
    Emojis and predefined messages allow participants
    to contribute reactions and commentary alongside
    gameplay, comparable to the additional text and
    commentary permitted by Square Enix.

    \item \textbf{Noncommercial Use:}
    Silima does not charge participants or monetize
    gameplay broadcasts.
\end{itemize}

We believe these characteristics align with Square Enix's
recognition of gameplay broadcasting accompanied by
original contributions and commentary.

However, Square Enix imposes restrictions on organizational
use and permitted broadcasting platforms. Its guidelines
are also specific to individual games and versions.

Consequently, while these policies support our
interpretation of Silima's broadcasting functionality,
they do not establish explicit authorization for
school-hosted broadcasts or older versions of the games.
