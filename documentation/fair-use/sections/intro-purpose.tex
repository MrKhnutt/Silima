\section{Introduction and Purpose}

Silima is a self-hosted platform designed to facilitate
collective gameplay through a browser-based interface.
It is primarily intended for recreational use in
educational environments, including classroom downtime,
rewards, and remediation activities.

This document outlines the reasoning behind our belief
that Silima's intended functionality is consistent with
established video game broadcasting practices and
published publisher policies.

\subsection{Our Position}

Silima is designed to function as a locally hosted
gameplay broadcasting platform. Although it operates
over a local network rather than through a public
service such as Twitch or YouTube, the fundamental
activity of transmitting gameplay to an audience
is technically comparable.

Many video game publishers explicitly permit gameplay
broadcasting, streaming, and sharing under established
guidelines. We believe these policies provide a
reasonable basis for evaluating Silima under the same
general framework, subject to the specific conditions
imposed by each publisher.

Silima is intended for noncommercial use and does
not distribute copyrighted game software to
participating devices.

\subsection{Scope and Limitations}

This document examines:
\begin{itemize}
    \item The technical similarities between Silima
          and established gameplay broadcasting platforms.
    \item Published broadcasting and content-sharing
          policies from major video game publishers.
    \item The applicability and limitations of these
          policies to locally hosted gameplay.
    \item The responsibilities associated with using
          commercial video games through Silima.
\end{itemize}

Silima does not distribute commercial game software,
ROMs, BIOS files, or other third-party copyrighted
game content. Games are obtained and executed
separately on the host computer.

This document presents a reasoned interpretation of
publicly available publisher policies. It does not
constitute legal advice or establish that every
publisher explicitly authorizes Silima.

Individual publishers may impose restrictions on
broadcasting platforms, institutions, or permitted
activities. Hosts remain responsible for complying
with applicable laws, licensing terms, and publisher
requirements.
