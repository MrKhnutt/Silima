\section{Conclusion}

Silima is designed to function as a locally hosted
gameplay broadcasting and interaction platform.
Although its implementation differs from conventional
services such as Twitch and YouTube, its intended
broadcasting functionality serves a substantially
similar technical purpose.

Based on established industry practices and the
published broadcasting policies of major video game
publishers, we believe there is a reasonable basis
for evaluating Silima under the same general guidelines
that govern conventional gameplay streaming.

Silima's noncommercial operation, limited audience,
interactive participation, and separation of game
execution from broadcasting further support this
interpretation.

However, technical similarity does not establish
automatic authorization. Individual publisher policies
may impose restrictions concerning platforms, content,
creative contributions, or institutional use.

Accordingly, we believe Silima can be used consistently
with existing gameplay broadcasting policies where
the applicable publisher's requirements are satisfied.
This document does not assert universal permission
to broadcast copyrighted game content.

The host remains responsible for ensuring that
individual games and their associated content are
obtained and used in accordance with applicable
publisher policies, licensing terms, and copyright law.
