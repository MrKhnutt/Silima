\section{Publisher Broadcasting Policies}

The following sections examine the publicly available
gameplay broadcasting and content-sharing policies of
major video game publishers.

Each analysis identifies the publisher's stated permissions,
relevant restrictions, and their potential applicability
to Silima's locally hosted broadcasting model.

These assessments are based on published policies rather
than direct authorization from the respective publishers.
Where a policy does not explicitly address Silima's
intended functionality, that uncertainty is acknowledged.
